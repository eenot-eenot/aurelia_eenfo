***Aurelian "democracy" cannot truly be called a democracy. No, it is a poor imitation. If democracy means "rule of the people", yet half of the people cannot vote, then clearly it is not the rule of the people. The inherent sexism deep ingrained in Aurelian society rears its head in the form of the voting structure, in which women are not allowed to choose their own path. If the idea is that "women are lesser in mind", then why does the king allow women to lead and rule factions? Clearly, it is not the woman herself which is the issue for the apologists of this discrimination. Rather, it is the fear. The fear that when women are able to choose who rule them, that they will break their shackles. That when they can decide with as much as power as the man, then they will push their own ideas forward. A woman can clearly lead, but only with the support of the men. Such is the system which has been built. Another reason for why the reactionaries and bourgeoisie wish to silence women, is that they are aware that when women are allowed to speak, they will criticise the conservatives who had oppressed them for so long, and join leagues with the working class. When they become educated and become policy makers, they will learn of who their oppressor is, and recognize that their struggle is not limited to them and their immediate surroundings, but towards the wider establishment which seeks to keep them in chains. Indeed, capitalism derives free labour from preventing women from speaking of their oppression within their homes. Thus, the conservatives afraid of consequence of actions, and the bourgeoisie afraid of losing their final source of truly free household labour, join forces to oppress women. Rest assured, our opinion in the DSD is firmly for the liberation of women. The AWL shares our sentiment, and together we will fight for the rights of 99%, and not for the right of only the 48.5%.***
