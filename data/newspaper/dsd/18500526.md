***Election polls are finally hear, the nation holds is breath! The AWL has received over 20% of the vote, and a staggering 42 seats! It has now become the second biggest party, despite targeted hate campaigns from reactionaries paid for by the bourgeoisie. Ahead of the AWL by a slim margin is the APAP, with 49 seats. István Masrl commented, "Despite the clear servitude to the bourgeoisie, I would still like to congratulate APAP for winning due to their strong commitment to democratic values and our ideological alignment on certain issues". The other conservative parties had a scramble for the seats, getting roughly equal portions of the remaining 89 seats. We attribute the conservative loss to vote splitting from the party splits occurring on very short notice ahead of the elections. Despite all odds stacked against them, workers and poor communities showed up on record numbers to vote for the AWL. The AWL has reiterated their commitment to strengthen democracy, vote against the bourgeois dictatorship, and to attain equality for all. Aurelia now becomes one of the first open and free democracies of the world, but the fairness of the elections remain uncertain, with many AWL politicians claiming intimidation of voters, restrictions on transport, and more. We will delve more into this on later issues. Glory to the working class!***
