***For decades the peasants and borderline serfs who had worked on the harsh farmlands in Agrinentan had one hope, and that was the protection of the king from the tyrannical nobles. That help never came. For hundreds of years now, we have been sold the lie that monarchs were the neutral guardians of the poor, that they would save the working class from the evil local lords. But has this worked? If these kings were supposedly so just, we would expect the peasants who worked on farmland to be incredibly prosperous, for they are the hardest working members of society. But this is not the case. Living standards and accommodations for farm workers in Agrinentan are subpar, even by low standards. Taxes and tithes take away most of the wealth that they gain. And due to the lack of industrialization and state driven innovation, the farmers' wealth are not low, they might be doing worse than before! Our analysts combed over the data over decades, and found that income had reduced by 12% when adjusting for inflation, that living conditions are steadily declining, and due to the neglect of the state, industrialization has not given them the bounty it was supposed to. We can expect the results to be accurate, since feudalism ended quite recently. It is clear to us that the king is not some "Hero of the Poor", rather, he operates in tandem with the corrupt government and the tyrant nobles. It was not after a revolution that this system had changed. It was only after we as the working class campaigned for our rights, that the feudal system was broken. Yet even now, the state has turnt a blind eye to the plight of the poor in Agrinentan, and not invested into its industrialization. We must learn that the king and his state apparatus is not good by default, rather, it is a structure meant to be replaced by the working class. We cannot trust nobles, monarchs, or the corrupt bureaucrats, a change must come. We believe only socialism is capable of liberating us from this stagnation. Workers, especially farmers, and especially farmers in Agrinentan, will stand to benefit from supporting socialism, only a socialist state can end the stagnation and decline.***
