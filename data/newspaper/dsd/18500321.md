***Ahead of the elections in a few months, the Aurelian Workers' League must find its footing on the issues presented before parliament. For that purpose, the leaders of all the 4 factions had met in the Sumf Conference Building once more, to decide their opinions on issues. It is expected for them to have decided on a full programme within a week from now, as predicted by experts close to all 4 figures. What may this programme, which István Masrl calls "The Iracten Programme" look like? While there is no definitive answer, it is likely that they will be for political and social reform, however, we are unsure of the other issues at hand. The other parties seem to be taking their stands too, with the APAP having decided its stance already. In any case, this election season is likely to bring a complete socialist victory, for the working class is with the AWL!***
