***"As mentioned in earlier publishes, the death toll after a factory fire in Ocidanten has now risen to over 125, with hundreds being injured. Despite this abhorrent violation of rights and safety, the wretched crooks who owned the factories did not provide compensation ahead of a measly sum to a few dozen injured. This outrageous act has been met with full force from several unions and the AWL Labour Wing, with Istvan Masrl himself condemning the owners, and declaring complete support to the strikes. Around 9000 workers across 22 factories are expected to quit work on the morning of 15th of March, and will continue their strike until, as declared by the union joint-action-committee, the individuals responsible for the tragedy are formally punished and compensation given to the victims. At De Soziali Demokrati, we support this show of resistance against oppression, and call on our readers to support the strike if they are able to. We the workers are the base of society and our rights must be respected."***
