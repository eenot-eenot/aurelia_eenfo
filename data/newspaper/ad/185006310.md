Female led protests organized by several female socialists and members of the National Women's Coalition have begun in Agrinenten, women are reportedly walking out of their homes and peacefully taking to the street to show their silent demand for the right to vote, several Textile Mills and tailor shops have been temporarily shuttered due to their labor refusing the work because of the protests.
It has even been rumored, though not verified, that several teachers have led their child students out of the classroom and onto the street to either observe or take part in the protests, though these rumors are not verified several parents have regardless withdrawn their children from schooling in Agrines to protect them from what they deem as radical ideology.
