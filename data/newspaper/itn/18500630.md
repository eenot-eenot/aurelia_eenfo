The elections have ended with no clear majority by one party but the APAP has secured the most seats at nearly 50. King Arthas II has nominated Alexander Von Hartmann for the chancellor and the first government is looking likely to be established as there is no clear opposition forming in Parliament and the party nominated is fairly moderate compared to other parties like the PCM or the AWL (who gained a surprising 42 seats). Upon asking capitalist representative Mr. Inkana from the UCFP what he thought about the chancellor’s government, he was confused and didn’t know who the chancellor was and he eventually said this, “Well uh I’ll get ta ya in a hour”. We aren’t sure what this means. 

Regardless, there are rumors of a bill being drafted by the PCM but little has been heard about what it contains. Chairman Schring had this to say when In The News asked him, “I cannot say what our plans are but I would like to say that this bill shall work for everyone. I, and the rest of my party, are open to speaking with all parties regardless of their radical beliefs or not.” Critics say calling other parties radical while claiming to want to work with them in the same sentence was hypocritical but we will ultimately see what happens whether or not the bill comes to light.
