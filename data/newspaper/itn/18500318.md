-# 3/18/1850
> In recent news, a major wing of the UCFP, the Schring Conservatives, has split from the party. A quote from the bloc leader, Edward Schring himself, clarifies why he initiated the split, “Look, it’s very simple, we weren’t being fairly represented. Mr. Gutenguzav repeatedly went behind our backs without consulting us on major policy decisions despite only being a bloc leader as I am.” Confusion and misaligning policies seem to have been the nail in the coffin for the party as current bloc leader Hanz Gutenguzav  corroborates this information, stating, “I slowly began changing ideals and so did my bloc, this caused that Schring wasn’t represented anymore.” Political scientist Auol Ciaco had predicted the split long ago stating, “The Schring Conservatives were never on the same page as the capitalists and Uberreichadvacements and it was idiotic to assume they could share a party in the first place. Big tent parties like this haven’t worked in the past and they won’t suddenly work now.” Another newspaper named the Gresafan has falsely accused Edward Schring of becoming a “progressive capitalist” and that was the reason for the split. There is 0 evidence of this being true and we condemn this use of disinformation. 

> In other news, the factory strike in Ocidanten is currently 3 days underway with support across the political spectrum as socialist parties had already given full support for the strike long ago but in an unusual move, the chairman of the new Conservative Party named the PCM has stated that, “While we don’t believe the massive escalation by the unions into a full blown strike with 9,000 workers is helping anybody, we do support their right to fight for basic working conditions. A factory fire that killed, what, like 150 people, is ever acceptable in a civilized nation like ours.”
