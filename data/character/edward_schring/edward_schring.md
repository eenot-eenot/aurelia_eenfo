Edward Schring is a famous conservative politician who was born in 1771 in Iracterie. He has dedicated his life to spreading his version of conservatism named, “Schring-Conservatism” which believes that democracy naturally brings destruction and a monarch is the only stable government structure. Additionally, it believes that tradition must be preserved, no matter what. 

He was born into a wealthy farming family but quickly turned to politics and traveled across the political spectrum in his early career as he started as a libertarian, then a capitalist, until he finally discovered and created Schring-Conservatism. 

He is currently leading the Schring Bloc in the United Coalition for Freedom & Prosperity  (UCFP) and owns half of the newspaper, In The News.
