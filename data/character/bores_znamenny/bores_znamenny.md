Born: ＿＿1828
Province: Soralenten
Political role: Member of the AWL, leader of the Radical-Republicanist wing, leader of the YSL

Born into the family of a foreign industrialist who had relocated to Sorales to develop steel and railway businesses, he received his early education at home, completed his studies at a regional gymnasium, and subsequently enrolled in the university in the capital to study economics, where he demonstrated strong academic performance.

Through rallies organized by the AWAA, he was introduced to socialist ideas and became deeply engaged with socialist literature. One of the first works he read being *Analysis of the Working Conditions of Aurelia*. He founded the Young Socialist League (YSL), an organization dedicated to studying socialist thought and disseminating these ideas among the youth. During the revolutionary events of 1848, he was detained by the police, spent six months in exile, and was expelled from the university. Shortly after the formation of the AWL, seeking like-minded socialists, he joined the party and rose to lead its Radical-Republicanist wing.

Currently, in addition to heading the YSL, he is engaged in the scholarly study of socialism and economics, as well as in political writing and agitation.

He possesses a gift of oratory talent, though he can sometimes be difficult to understand due to his accent and gaps in his command of the language.
