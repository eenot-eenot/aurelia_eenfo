Born: unknown, 1819
Home region: Provencie Sacs
Ideology: Corpratist Liberalism

Life story:
Born into a wealthy mining family, Alexander was raised by his father and his servants.
His father would raise him with strict discipline, shaping him for life as a middle manager at mine near the border of Anzaria. He would teach Alexander his role as an owner and his "special position" in the eyes of the lord over the serfs.

With his mother dying during his birth, his father would blame him for her death, straining their relationship.
As a result he would often be punished severely for arbitrary reasons, as well as being forced to work alongside the serfs in his families mines, giving him unusual sympathy for "working peoples".

His political awakening would come in 1838, when the workers of one of his fathers mines launched a strike that lead to a shootout that left 10 dead. 
In the shootout, one of his close cousins would die, forcing him to take a much higher position within his families organization and help his aging father with the business.
He would also shift his position on the feudal system as he would face pressure from local lords over land rights near his mines.

Actually important part:
With this he would form his own ideology, using his understanding of history and privileged position to create his theory of class relationship and greater society.
He believes that every person belongs to a certain class, with some being naturally higher than others, though with some social mobility being possible. He believes that the cooperation of these classes is the main precursor to healthy society, with "social anarchy" being the greatest threat.

Taking part in the 1848 riots, Alexander would side firmly with the liberals in the short term to "balance the scales", conveniently targeting lords who he had land disputes with.
His main goal is to handle the "societal shift" brought on by the riots and allow the rise of (preferably slightly limited) capitalism.
