# Backstory
Olenna Rozemberg is the wealthy Duchess of Adrinan in Anzaria, she was born in Agrines on March 16th 1786 into the heavily inbred House of Agrinoa, she was the daughter of Lord Agrinoa, Count of Agrines. At a young age 3 of her 4 siblings were lost to the Plague, of which they were susceptible to due to their weakened immune systems caused from decades of inbreeding, her only sibling left was her infertile older sister Marinka who later succeeded their father as Countess of Agrines.

 at the age of 16 in 1802 she was married to the only son of the Duke of Adrinan and moved to Anzaria. At first she struggled to adjust to the new environment, but she quickly gathered herself and vowed not to submit to the role of a political coupon for her father.

She spent the following years building her base in Anzaria, collecting allies in the castle and influencing her Husband, while bearing 2 daughters. Her new father in law, The Duke of Adrinan himself taught her much of her core philosophy, not through actual training but by his reckless, prideful, and violent behavior, he would often insult those which were critical to the duchies operations based on his whims, challenge other nobles to duels to the death based on petty squabbles, and overall make enemies of himself across the realm from his outrageous outbursts.
Watching this man seemingly tear him and his entire family down by acting only on, as she viewed, pure manly rage, pride, and greed significantly laid the foundations of her matriarchical ideology. As she viewed it, these were largely male emotions which could be perceived damaging society and progress across every single village, castle, shack, and city from Aurelia to The Gamleat.

In 1816 "tragedy" befell her new family as the Duke of Adrinan, her father in law, died brutally infront of the entire castle in a savage brawl which he sparked during a feast. She watched as he boiled over with anger and pride and let it consume him and corrupt his judgement, all over the last chicken wing...
Following the death of her father in law her Husband succeeded his father as the Duke of Adrinan, for Olenna this was wonderful as she had spent half of her life preparing for this, manipulating her husband, gaining contacts and allies, growing her silent influence over the Duchy.

Following the accession of the new Duke it was revealed to all that the previous Dukes lavish partying and spending had near bankrupted the house, at first she merely guided her Husbands attempts to rectify the issue but soon was able to convince him to directly appoint her as the financial advisors over the entire Duchy.
