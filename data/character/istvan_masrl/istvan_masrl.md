Born: April 11th, 1816
Ideology: Utopian Socialism
Leader of: All-Workers Association of Aurelia
Alma Mater: University of Auriele
Notable ideas: *The Baseline of Standards*
Province: Aurenten

Born to a middle class family near the old Anzarian capital Adrinan, Istvan became a well known defense lawyer after graduating from law school in Aurelia. During his years in university, he took interest in the books regarding workers plights and became a socialist. He, along with many of his peers, formed the AWAA to fight for the working class. Eventually, after the death of an association member, he and his organization became more hardline socialists. During 1848, he was imprisoned numerous times for helping the liberal revolutionaries, but was able to go free due to his training in the judicial system and the wide support he enjoyed amongst the AWAA. During that period, he defended many revolutionaries in court, and developed his network among the revolutionaries. He and the AWAA helped organise general labour strikes during the time, which were not very successful. Istvan, having devoted much of his time and effort into the 1848 revolutions, did not want to waste the democracy they had recieved. He believed workers could win elections and take over the state, and then push socialist values into the nation. As such, he and AWAA, along with Tristan's SMWWC and Wetz's ADO, founded the Aurelian Workers' League, to carry the legacy of the revolution forward. 
Masrl is known for his fiery speeches, while also being able to negotiate and not be as stubborn as other AWAA members. He wrote his magnum opus as "Analysis of the Working Conditions of Aurelia", which stated that under capitalism, the working conditions will always be the bare minimum, and coordinated disobedience and public demands could very marginally increase the bare minimum (as seen with the abolition of slavery), though he states that it will remain quite poor and only socialism can truly give better conditions.
