# Backstory

Cyrie "Silenne" Arenne is an Aurelian lawyer, political reformer, and Co-founder of the Aurelian People's Action Party. Born into a Upper Middle Class Family in 1824, She received an education in law and political Activism before entering public life.

Following the constitutional upheavals of 1848, Silenne became increasingly involved in reformist politics, advocating industrialization, Administrative efficiency, meritocracy, and Social Equality, And Anti-Corruption

In 1850, She Co-Founded the Aurelian People's Action Party, establishing a political movement dedicated to transforming Aurelia into a modern industrial and commercial power.

A Egalitarian and disciplined political figure, Advocates against constitutional monarchy while advocating effective Social institutions and accountable government.

Her political philosophy is centred on economic development, professional administration, national prosperity, and social advancement.

**Political motto:** Progress, Society, Progression.
