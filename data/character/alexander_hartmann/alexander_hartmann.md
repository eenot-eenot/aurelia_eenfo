**Backstory **

Alexander Friedrich von Hartmann is an Aurelian lawyer, political reformer, and founder of the Aurelian People's Action Party. Born into a prosperous merchant family in 1822, he received an education in law and political economy before entering public life.

Following the constitutional upheavals of 1848, Hartmann became increasingly involved in reformist politics, advocating industrialization, administrative efficiency, meritocracy, and economic modernization.

In 1850, he founded the Aurelian People's Action Party, establishing a political movement dedicated to transforming Aurelia into a modern industrial and commercial power.

A pragmatic and disciplined political figure, Hartmann supports constitutional monarchy while advocating effective parliamentary institutions and accountable government.

His political philosophy is centred on economic development, professional administration, national prosperity, and social advancement.

**Political motto:** Progress, Unity, Prosperity.
