**Party Constitution:**
The party officially designates Edward Schring as the permanent chairman of the party which gives him almost unlimited authority over the party. Although, an unspoken rule exists where he must allow debate over new laws and bills and mustn’t abuse his power. 

Due to the loyalty and very close ideologies of all the members, they practically have unanimous support for new laws. 

*Party Beliefs:*
Tradition - Tradition is easily the most important thing to the party’s beliefs. In this context, tradition refers to keeping and never changing the culture, customs, and institutions of the nation, no matter what. 
Isolationism - The nation mustn’t involve itself in foreign affairs that do not directly involve our nation though trade is an exception. 
Capitalism - The party believes in up-keeping the current capitalist economic system though this is not a priority of the party. 
Monarchism - Through Schring-Conservatism, the only way to keep a nation stable is through a monarchy. The party wishes to dissolve the new democracy in the near future.

*Motto:* *"Live, Love, And Die For Tradition."*
