**Party Constitution:**
*The Matriarchal Womens Coalition is under the exclusive domination of the Honorable House Rozemberg-Agrinoa of the Duchy of Adrinan and the County of Agrines. The political program is formed via the internal meetings of our house which have present a small advisory committee made up of the common constituents which we represent. The decisions of the House Rozemberg-Agrinoa are published weekly in the National Statute by Her Grace Olenna Rozemberg after the unanimous agreement of the house.*



*Ideology*: Conservative Matriarchal Monarchism. A conservative society with women elevated as leading figures in preference over men, increased autonomy for nobles, nationalist as in __IN FAVOR OF AND PROTECTIVE OF THE NATION STATE NOT NAZISM!!__  Noble influence/ownership on economics.
*Political leaning*: Socially and nationally Right-Leaning and old glory Conservative
*Motto*: "Glory Through The Queen."
*Extras*: || Inspired union between the two parties from Aurelia 1: The Aurelia Sovereignty Front (ASF) and the Matriarchal Nationalist Party (MNP) ||
*Formed on* August 22nd 1839 in Agrines
*Foreign policy*: Isolationism except commerce. Aurelia stays out of foreign affairs and focuses on its scientific glory and the prosperity of its people, highly focused on building its youth and restructuring female society. War is only necessary when in our defense, conquest is a barbaric male attitude.
*Economic policy*: Royal Social Capitalism. Private business is free and open to all citizens, but the crown and nobility directly own and have a stake in many monopolistic companies in key sectors (energy, transport, banking, defence) just like the days of old __this is not radical collectivization, rather a continuation of the past__. Instead of solely funding the indulgences of noble men profits will fund free services for every citizen: healthcare, education, housing support, public transport, and childcare and maternal support. Benefits are reserved for citizens. The market is free, but the Crown is its biggest player in order to maintain order, fairness, and proper motherly direction over the society.
